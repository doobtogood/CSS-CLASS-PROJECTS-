Minecraft is an open-world block-building sandbox game developed by Mojang Studios and originally created by Markus "Notch" Persson

Overview

  • Gameplay: Players explore a procedurally generated 3D world made of blocks, gathering resources, crafting tools, building structures, and fighting or interacting with creatures.

  • Survival: Players must gather resources, manage hunger and health, and defend against hostile monsters.

  • Creative: Players receive unlimited resources, flight capability, and immunity to damage to focus purely on building.

  • Other Modes: Includes Hardcore (a single-life survival challenge), Adventure, and Spectator.
